Common questions about SMS integrations for teams

How do SMS integrations work with my existing tools?+

Mobile Text Alerts connects to your tools either through native integrations (direct connections built into the platform) or through Zapier (which acts as a bridge to 5,000+ apps). Once connected, you set trigger conditions. For example: "When a card moves to Done in Trello, send an SMS to the project manager." Setup typically takes a few minutes per integration, and no coding is required.

Can I send SMS to my team and customers from the same platform?+

Yes. Mobile Text Alerts supports both internal notifications (alerting your team about events in your tools) and external messaging (sending texts to customers and clients). You manage both from a single dashboard, with separate contact lists, automation rules, and compliance settings for each.

Is there a limit to how many integrations I can connect?+

No. You can connect as many integrations as your workflow requires. Most teams start with one or two (typically their project management tool and scheduling app) and expand from there.

Do I need technical skills to set up these integrations?+

No. Both native integrations and Zapier connections use visual, no-code setup flows. You select a trigger event (something happening in your tool), define the SMS content, choose the recipients, and activate. Mobile Text Alerts also offers free 1-on-1 onboarding demos.

What does SMS cost per message?+

Mobile Text Alerts offers plans based on message volume, starting with a free trial that includes credits to test your integrations. Pricing scales with usage, so you only pay for what you send. Visit the pricing page for current plan details.
